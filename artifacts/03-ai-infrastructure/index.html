SELECTED · 01

Approved company policies

Only owner-approved, current policy documents are eligible. Restrict sensitive HR records, personal data, and confidential leadership material from the initial pilot.

Accountable: HR and IT policy owners

Two safeguards built into the flow

Permission before retrieval: authenticate each employee and apply source-document access controls before any content reaches the LLM. Freshness before citation: only approved and currently effective versions should be cited; unresolved conflicts go to a policy owner.

Human review stays in charge

The assistant can summarize policy, but it cannot create policy or make sensitive HR decisions. It should flag ambiguity, link its sources, and route unresolved issues to the accountable expert.

02 / GOVERNANCE & OPERATIONS

Built for reliability. Governed for trust.

Operational safeguards are part of the architecture—not features to add after launch.

01 · IDENTITY

Least-privilege access

Enterprise sign-in, role- and document-level permissions, and pre-retrieval filtering. Unauthorized passages must never enter model context.

Encryption + retention

Encrypt traffic and storage. Minimize chat content in logs; retain or delete chats, indexes, and audit events under an approved policy.

One business day

Sync approved policy changes within one business day. Track version, effective date, document owner, and approval status; quarantine conflicts.

04 · MONITORING

Detect, respond, improve

Monitor access violations, groundedness, broken citations, synchronization delays, service health, and cost. Maintain an incident response and rollback plan.

Approval before confidential data

IT/Security, policy owners, privacy/legal, and procurement must review the proposed use. Verify vendor data-use, retention, subprocessors, incident response and contract terms. A vendor’s advertised feature is not an institutional approval.

03 / PLATFORM DECISION

Why managed RAG over fine-tuning?

RECOMMENDED FOR CONTROLLED PILOT✓

Managed RAG + approved LLM API

Retrieve live, permission-filtered documents without training a new foundation model. Maintain a document ingestion/indexing pipeline, embeddings, a vector/retrieval store, and LLM inference.

Advantages Faster policy updates, source citations, lower in-house platform burden.
Tradeoffs API latency, usage cost, vendor dependency, indexing/monitoring work.
ALTERNATIVE

Fine-tuning or fully self-hosting

Fine-tuning may improve output style or domain behavior, but it does not inherently enforce source permissions or keep answers current. Self-hosting provides more direct infrastructure control.

Disadvantages for this pilot Higher engineering, compute, and maintenance requirements. A custom fine-tuned model still needs source governance and security controls.
COMPUTE

Embedding/indexing jobs and LLM inference

PLATFORM

Managed hosting, vector storage, integration

PEOPLE

CHANGE

Staff training, communication, policy updates

04 / RELEASE DECISION

Ready to design. Not ready to deploy.

The simulated access failures make a production launch unjustified. Continue only with synthetic/approved data and controlled evaluation until permission enforcement and freshness are demonstrated.

!
PROPOSED STATUS: POSTPONE REAL DEPLOYMENTNo production use of protected records until tests and approvals pass.
OUR SYSTEM MAY

Answer authorized policy questions with current source citations and flag uncertainty for human review.

OUR SYSTEM MUST NOT

Expose restricted content, fabricate policy, or independently make sensitive personnel decisions.

PAUSE / ROLLBACK / RETIRE IF

Any unauthorized disclosure occurs, approved updates miss the one-business-day window, required approvals are absent, or repeated unresolved failures make the design unsafe.

REFLECTION

A model can answer a question. A system must earn trust.

This design taught me that successful AI deployment depends as much on data ownership, access boundaries, and accountability as on model capability. The proposed architecture makes the human decision points visible rather than treating a generated answer as final authority.
